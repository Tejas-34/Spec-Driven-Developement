The UI style should feel like a modern SaaS product used by developers, students, and creators for deep work and productivity tracking.

Overall Design Style:
- Dark mode only
- Futuristic and minimal
- Clean glassmorphism interface
- Neon gradient glow effects
- Soft shadows and blurred backgrounds
- Modern typography
- Smooth hover animations
- Spacious layout with proper padding
- Professional and premium look

- Background: deep navy blue / black gradient
- Accent colors:
  - Cyan
  - Purple
  - Neon blue
  - Pink glow
- Cards should have:
  - semi-transparent glass effect
  - subtle borders
  - blurred background
  - glowing hover effects

- App logo at top (“FocusSprint”)

  - Dashboard

- Greeting message:
  “Good evening, Tejas 👋”

- Gradient glow border
- Hover animation
